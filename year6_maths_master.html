Maths Keywords

Learn the important words for Year 6.

Fractions

Numerator: the top number; how many parts you have.

Denominator: the bottom number; how many equal parts make the whole.

Equivalent: fractions with the same value.

Simplify: make a fraction smaller without changing its value.

Proper fraction: a fraction less than one whole.

Decimals

Decimal point: separates whole numbers from decimal parts.

Tenths: parts divided into 10 equal pieces.

Hundredths: parts divided into 100 equal pieces.

Place value: the value of a digit because of its position.

Percentages

Percent: means “out of 100”.

Discount: an amount taken off a price.

Increase: when an amount gets larger.

Decrease: when an amount gets smaller.

Convert: change a number from one form to another.
